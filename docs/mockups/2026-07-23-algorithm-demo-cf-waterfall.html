Locked design, 2026-07-23. Chosen from three waterfall treatments; the deciding factor was that every quantity is read off its own labelled scale rather than encoded in bar thickness, so the panel can verify each number without decoding a visual channel. Sample data is arithmetically consistent with recommender/CF.py and recommender/cbf.py.
