Schedule time clarity

Three AM / PM treatments for weekly availability

These keep the existing StudyBuddy schedule structure, but remove the current ambiguity around entries like 01:30 - 02:00 by making the period visible at scan speed.

My pick: Design 1. It is the smallest change, fits the current pill layout, and makes every slot clear without adding more vertical groups.

Design 1: Explicit AM / PM Pills

Converts each slot to 12-hour time and adds a high-contrast AM or PM chip on the right. Best when we want maximum clarity with minimal layout disruption.
