Index rows

No cards at all. Each tool is a full-width editorial row — hollow outlined numeral, title, copy — divided by hairlines like the journey panels. Rows slide in from the left; hovering fills the numeral and tints the whole row.

Horizontal rail

The section pins and your vertical scroll drives the row sideways — four wide tinted slabs, each over half the viewport wide, glide through. Numerals are big italic serif watermarks. The most "studio site" of the three.

Option C

Full-bleed bands
